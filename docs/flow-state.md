# The Flow State

Malicia (common) and Raven (rare) join the style roster. The Characters playbook
shows each move's actual on-ball/off-ball version, awakening and passive.

Open **Flows** to view all twenty collectible Flows and equip one you own. A
normal Flow spin costs $26.25; lucky costs $71.25, exactly 25% below style spins.
Both kinds of lucky spin require level 15. Undisputed is Flow-only: 0.01% normal
and 0.1% lucky; those weights come from common and rare respectively.

Press **X** or the Flow button at 20% or more. Duration is the meter divided by
two in seconds, from 10s at 20% to 50s at 100%. Activation consumes the meter and
cannot be cancelled. Only the active Flow shows its aura. New meter gains can
build toward the next activation. Character and DLSI synergies are listed on
every Flow card. Raw Skill and penalty modes keep their character-free rules.

- Ankle break: +10%; steal: +5%; assist: +25%; goal: +45%.
- Lose possession to a steal: -20%; get ankle-broken: -10%.
- Flow passes and shots stay distinct. Magician's Magic Cast stops contact
  interceptions but allows skill interception; DLSI Playmaker's Elitist Awareness
  stops both. Neither protection is added to ordinary kicks.

Each gameplay second gives 2 XP for your own or a teammate's possession, 1 XP for
a loose ball or either goalkeeper's possession, and 0 during rival possession.
Cinematics, replays and celebrations give no XP. Level 1→2 costs 200 XP, then
300, 400, etc. Level 15 begins at 11,900 total XP. Abandoned matches award no XP.

Account results use replayed possession time, not caller-supplied XP, and settle
once using the existing receipt. Device progress remains separate. Existing
matches replay with their exact published engine. New wallet cents occupy the
existing game-profile JSON alongside its existing whole-dollar bigint; no new
schema, credential, role, account or production migration is required.
